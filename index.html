About Me

With over 15 years of experience in architectural design, I specialize in creating innovative spaces that reflect both the client's vision and the surrounding environment. My work focuses on sustainable design principles and modern aesthetics.

I believe that architecture should not only be visually stunning but also functional, sustainable, and tailored to the needs of those who inhabit the space.

Paradise Pearl Hall

Project Overview

The Royal Wedding Hall is a premium event space designed for luxury weddings and celebrations. Located in the heart of the city, this venue combines traditional Pakistani architectural elements with contemporary design.

Key Features

  • Grand entrance with ornate archways
  • Spacious main hall accommodating 500+ guests
  • Custom crystal chandeliers and lighting
  • State-of-the-art sound and visual systems
  • Separate dining and ceremony areas
  • Elegant facade with traditional motifs

Materials & Finishes

  • Italian marble flooring
  • Custom woodwork and carvings
  • Gold leaf accents
  • Textured wall panels
  • Premium fabric drapery

Grand Celebration Hall

Project Overview

The Grand Celebration Hall is designed as a versatile event space suitable for weddings, corporate events, and social gatherings. The design focuses on creating a memorable experience through architectural details and spatial planning.

Key Features

  • Modular space with movable partitions
  • Advanced lighting control systems
  • Custom-designed wall panels with intricate patterns
  • Spacious outdoor area for pre-function events
  • VIP lounge with premium amenities
  • Eco-friendly design with energy-efficient systems

Design Elements

  • Contemporary interpretation of traditional motifs
  • Warm color palette with gold accents
  • Textured surfaces creating visual interest
  • Strategic use of natural light
  • Custom furniture designed specifically for the space
